# Style-study provenance

This public repository contains reusable style notes and corrected measurements from a user-supplied figure. The user's Zotero library, downloaded paper text and reference screenshots are not bundled.

The local collection was not verified as ten Nature/Science flagship articles. Do not cite it as such or infer universal journal conventions from it. Treat the style profile as a working design prior; verify journal, DOI and figure provenance before adding future papers to an evidence-based corpus.

See [corrected measurements](reference-measurements-2026-10-22.md) and [user conventions](../user-figure-conventions.md).
